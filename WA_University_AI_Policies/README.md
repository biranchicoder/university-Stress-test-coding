# Western Australia university AI policy research

Official university and university-library AI, generative-AI, assessment, research, and academic-integrity sources collected on 5 November 2026.

## Coverage

- **Curtin University** — policy PDFs plus seven webpage captures. See `Curtin_University/README.md`.
- **The University of Western Australia** — 2026 student guide, assessment guidance, and library AI guide.
- **Edith Cowan University** — student AI guidance, generative-AI learning page, and AI-literacy framework.
- **Murdoch University** — Student Academic Integrity Policy, including unauthorised AI use.
- **University of Notre Dame Australia** — Academic Integrity Policy and research-thesis AI guidance.

All sources are official university domains or official university CDN/library domains. Webpage captures are stored in each university's `Webpage_Captures/` folder.

## Important validation note

Some university PDF endpoints return access-denied HTML to command-line downloaders. The original direct PDF is retained where it downloaded successfully; a print-to-PDF capture is retained separately where Chromium could render the page. Files should be opened or text-extracted before quoting in a final report.
