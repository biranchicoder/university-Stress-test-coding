# Initial findings — Western Australia university AI sources

## Curtin University

- AI use in assessment is unit-specific and requires checking the unit coordinator's rules.
- Students are expected to verify AI output, avoid sensitive/confidential input, and disclose permitted use.
- Curtin's 2026 student guidance says Turnitin GenAI detection is disabled from 1 January 2026; academic-integrity review continues.
- Research guidance emphasises human oversight, intellectual integrity, privacy, and protection of intellectual property.

## The University of Western Australia

- UWA's 2026 student guide describes three assessment tiers: no AI, AI assistance, and fully embedded AI.
- Permission is determined by the unit coordinator or assessment instructions; permitted use must be acknowledged and, where required, cited/referenced.
- The guide requires students to retain responsibility for accuracy and for the final work being their own.

## Edith Cowan University

- ECU's public student guidance says AI may support learning, planning, explanations, feedback, and research directions, but must not replace the student's assessed work.
- Unauthorised AI-generated assessment work is treated as an academic-integrity breach; unit instructions determine what is permitted.
- ECU's teaching framework focuses on AI literacy, transparency, accountability, and explaining the student's own contribution.

## Murdoch University

- The Student Academic Integrity Policy lists using artificial intelligence without authorisation as a breach, specifically including submission of work produced in whole or part by AI where assessment requirements do not authorise it.
- The policy's listed review date is 27 March 2026; confirm whether a newer replacement exists before relying on it as current policy.

## University of Notre Dame Australia

- The Academic Integrity Policy defines unauthorised use of generative AI broadly, including writing assistance, grammar checking, translation, paraphrasing, and reference generators unless expressly allowed.
- The research-thesis guideline requires HDR students to consult supervisors before using AI/GenAI for thesis writing and to acknowledge the full nature and scope of use.

This is an initial evidence summary, not a legal interpretation. The source files and URLs in each university folder are the authoritative research record.
