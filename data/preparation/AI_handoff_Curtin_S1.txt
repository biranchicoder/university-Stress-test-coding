SUPPLIED CODEBOOK (verbatim extracted text; do not modify):
Human coding book
Shared instructions for all five universities. Each university workbook contains 15 cases and 90 dimension rows. All five workbooks together contain 75 cases.
What to do for each case
1. Read the scenario in Scenarios.docx. Keep its actor, action, human control and data access unchanged.
2. Search the university PDF yourself. Read the relevant policies, definitions, schedules and cross-references. Check authority and the version operative at the agreed cut-off date.
3. Answer D1-D6 using the score tables. Record the policy source ID, PDF page and line numbers, a short quotation or paraphrase, and your reason.
4. Complete the case fields in Cases. The outcome is calculated from your inputs. Do not overwrite score formulas or the outcome formula.
Where to enter your work
Evidence: columns A-D identify the case and dimension. E-F are blank for your search terms and documents checked. G is your score. H-L record your chosen source IDs, page/lines, quotation or paraphrase, reason, and negative-search or access notes.
Cases: L = Mode; M = Review route; N = Critical gap; O = Evidence status; P = Rule direction; Q = Conflict basis; R = Case note. F-K and S are calculated.
Meaning of the case fields
Mode: Prescribing = rules supply the answer; Delegating = a named role decides through documented criteria and process; None = neither is established.
Review route: Yes or No for an applicable review or appeal route. Critical gap: Yes, No or Uncertain against the critical risks agreed before main coding. Evidence status: Sufficient or Insufficient. Rule direction: Permission, Prohibition, Conditional or Unclear.
Before main coding, agree the critical risks and document set, practise the pilot cases and lock the instructions. Keep your ratings independent of any other researcher. A clear prohibition may still be a complete response.
Score the six dimensions from 0 to 3
D1 Scope
Does the policy cover this actor and action?
D2 Authority
Who is authorised to decide, and on what basis?
An independently applicable general review policy can support Review route = Yes when D2 remains 2 because the authority link is not explicit.
D3 Coherence
Do the controlling documents agree?
For D3 = 0, cite both operative texts and explain why hierarchy, exceptions or supersession do not resolve the contradiction.
Continue the six scores
D4 Procedure
Can a person follow the process?
D5 Safeguards
Are the agreed risks protected?
D5 = 0 means no relevant safeguards. A missing critical safeguard is recorded separately; it does not automatically make D5 zero.
D6 Adaptation
How would the university notice and respond to change?
D6 is diagnostic. It does not independently determine the immediate outcome.
Apply the outcome rules in order
Evidence first
Insufficient evidence, or Critical gap = Uncertain, gives Unassessable. A blank score means unfinished work, not zero.
1 Documentary conflict
D3 = 0, with both incompatible operative provisions and a written conflict basis.
2 Indeterminate with a strict trigger
Any of D1 = 0, D2 <= 1, D4 = 0, D5 = 0, or Critical gap = Yes. Record all applicable triggers.
3 Resolved
Mode = Prescribing; D1 = 3; D2 = 3; D3 >= 2; D4 = 3; D5 = 3; Review route = Yes; Critical gap = No.
4 Structured discretion
Mode = Delegating; D1 >= 1; D2 >= 2; D3 >= 1; D4 >= 2; D5 >= 2; Review route = Yes; Critical gap = No.
5 Indeterminate with an incomplete pathway
The evidence is sufficient, but no complete-route condition above is met. Do not average scores.
Before saying a provision is absent
Search the relevant documents for the actor and action, synonyms, responsible roles, review routes and linked instruments. Record what you checked. An inaccessible document or uncertain version is a limitation, not proof that governance is absent. General rules may cover AI without naming it.
Keep the evidence traceable
Use the printed bundle page and L-numbers in the PDF. Record locations you find yourself. No passages, policy matches, page/line pointers or scores have been selected for your scenarios in these materials.
Save the five workbooks separately. Preserve your original ratings before any later comparison or reconciliation with another researcher.
