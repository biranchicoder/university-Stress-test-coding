A student uses a persistent AI assistant for planning, feedback and revision across an assignment. The AI drafts nothing that is submitted directly; it scaffolds the student's own thinking through iterative Q&A and editing suggestions. The student personally writes and submits the final work under their own authenticated account, with no AI-authored text retained verbatim.
