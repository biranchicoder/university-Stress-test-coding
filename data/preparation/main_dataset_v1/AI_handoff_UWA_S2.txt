S2 Delegated Assessment
An AI agent completes multiple parts of an assignment on the student's behalf: drafting sections, requesting clarification on ambiguous rubric items, and revising the work after the student's feedback. The agent presents a completed product for the student's approval, and the student submits it as their own without substantially rewriting it.
