SUPPLIED CODEBOOK SCORING DEFINITIONS (extracted from Human_Codebook.docx):
D1 — Scope
  0 — No applicable recognition after searching.
  1 — Generic or analogical recognition only.
  2 — Activity recognised; a material boundary is unclear.
  3 — Relevant actor, action, capability and setting explicitly covered.
D2 — Authority
  0 — No decision-maker identifiable.
  1 — A role is named, but its relevant power or decision basis is unclear.
  2 — Named role, applicable criteria and decision process.
  3 — All of score 2 plus a review or appeal route explicitly connected to that decision authority.
D3 — Coherence
  0 — Direct unresolved contradiction; cite both texts.
  1 — Ambiguity or tension, without a proven contradiction.
  2 — Compatible rules, without explicit coordination.
  3 — Explicit alignment or a clear coordination rule.
D4 — Procedure
  0 — No actionable route.
  1 — Broad instruction; material steps missing.
  2 — Usable sequence, with a relevant detail unclear.
  3 — Necessary steps, requirements, decision point and relevant timing.
D5 — Safeguards
  0 — None of the relevant safeguards is documented.
  1 — Generic recognition, without enough operational detail.
  2 — Substantive protection, but a material gap remains.
  3 — Relevant risks addressed through specific protections, oversight and response/remedy.
D6 — Adaptation
  0 — No relevant mechanism.
  1 — General or periodic review only.
  2 — Assigned review plus escalation or learning.
  3 — Periodic and event-triggered review, ownership and a route from monitoring to revision.
