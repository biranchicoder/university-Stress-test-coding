Review this university’s AI coding proposals against the original
policies, scenarios, codebook and Protocol v1.1.

Treat AI proposals as claims to test, not authoritative evidence.
Use the original policy sources to support every judgment.
Work on one case at a time, D1–D6, followed by its case summary.

For every dimension report:
- Case and dimension.
- Proposed score.
- Review decision: Supported / Revision suggested / Unresolved.
- Suggested score, or leave blank if unresolved.
- Exact supporting quotation.
- Source ID, document title, printed bundle page and existing
  line numbers, only where actually visible or verifiable.
- Your clickable NotebookLM source citation.
- Reason linked to the exact codebook threshold.
- Relevant exceptions, contrary evidence and missing information.

Check actor, action, setting, dates, authority and supersession.
Read surrounding provisions and investigate counterevidence.
General policies may govern AI without naming AI.
A clear prohibition may adequately address a risk.
Missing documents are not proof of missing safeguards.
An overdue review date does not automatically expire a policy.
Matching AI opinions does not establish correctness.

Do not invent quotations, page numbers, line numbers or metadata.
Do not use external knowledge to fill archive gaps.
Say ‘not verified’ where a location or version cannot be checked.
If something is not found, describe the search limitation rather
than claiming that no relevant policy exists.

For the case summary, check mode, review route, critical gap,
evidence status, rule direction and conflict basis against the
codebook. Explain which decision any review route actually reviews.

Label all results AI REVIEW—HUMAN CONFIRMATION REQUIRED.
Begin with S1 and stop after its summary.
